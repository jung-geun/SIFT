# SIFT user guide

Start by writing down one task. Connect email, calendars, or AI only when you need them.

## Write down tasks and see what needs doing today

1. **Record a task as soon as it comes to mind.** Enter a title for a new task and save it. You can put the details in its notes.
2. **Add only the dates and priorities you need.** Tasks without deadlines can stay undated.
3. **Mark finished tasks as complete.** You can view active, overdue, and completed tasks separately.

Review items in the review queue before moving them to your active tasks. If a title or deadline is not right, you can edit it yourself.

## Turn email requests into tasks

1. **Connect your email account first.** Gmail connects over IMAP using an app password. Follow the steps in the [email setup guide](email-setup.html).
2. **Open an email and check the request.** Review the sender, body, and actual deadline.
3. **Use 'Save as To-Do' only for emails you need.** Review the title, notes, and deadline before saving. If no deadline is found, you can set one yourself or leave it blank.

Automatic email analysis lets you review required actions found in the body. Compare them with the original and explicitly review and save the items you need. Saving analysis results and creating a task are separate actions.

Marking an email as read or moving it to the trash is also reflected in your connected email account.

## See your schedule alongside tasks, with connections you choose

The calendar lets you view events and tasks together by date. When connecting your device's calendars, check the access permissions and which calendars to use.

Connecting calendars and automatically adding tasks as events are separate settings. Enable only the features you need. If you choose a shared calendar, also check the titles and notes that other people may see.

## After a meeting, keep the actions you need

1. **Confirm participants' consent before recording.** Choose the audio sources to record, or import an audio file you already have.
2. **Read the transcript and summary.** Available transcription and summary features depend on your device, language, and installed models.
3. **Choose actions to bring into your tasks.** Check their titles and deadlines in the review queue before moving them to your active tasks.

AI-generated transcripts, summaries, and tasks may contain errors or omissions. Check important names, numbers, and dates against the original audio or text.

## Manage recurring work with routines

Organize frequently repeated tasks into routines and check their frequency and timing yourself. When using recurring suggestions, review whether they fit your life before accepting them.

A completed task records that you checked it off. Do not treat it as confirmation that you actually exercised or took medication.

## Choose how to handle your data and connections

- **You can start basic task management without connecting an account.** Default processing takes place on your device.
- **iCloud sync is optional.** Enabling it on supported devices lets you share app data across the same Apple account. Email bodies and original meeting audio are stored separately on your device.
- **External AI connections are optional, too.** Connecting an account and consenting to share app data are separate choices. Check which information will be sent before allowing it.
- **Enable additional models and beta features only when you need them.** They may require download space or a supported device. Subscriptions and usage limits for external services are separate from the free app.

See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.html) for details on storage, transmission, and deletion.
